# Government Schemes Official Reference & Knowledge Base

This directory contains official documentation, guidelines, and PDF metadata for Indian central and state government schemes supporting rural and semi-urban micro-entrepreneurs.

## Real Official Sources & PDF Download Links

1. **PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)**
   - **Ministry**: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) & SIDBI
   - **Official Portal**: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/
   - **Scheme Guidelines PDF**: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/assets/doc/Scheme_Guidelines_English.pdf
   - **Key Benefits**:
     - 1st Tranche: ₹10,000 collateral-free working capital loan (1 year tenure).
     - 2nd Tranche: ₹20,000 on timely repayment of 1st loan.
     - 3rd Tranche: ₹50,000 on timely repayment of 2nd loan.
     - 7% per annum interest subsidy credited directly to bank account quarterly.
     - Cashback up to ₹1,200/year on digital transactions (₹1 per transaction).
   - **Eligibility**: Street vendors, peri-urban hawkers, small stall owners vending vegetables, fruits, tea, pakodas, snacks, kirana, and apparel.

2. **Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)**
   - **Ministry**: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME) & KVIC
   - **Official Portal**: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal/pmegphome/index.jsp
   - **Scheme Guidelines PDF**: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb/docs/Scheme_guidelines.pdf
   - **Key Benefits**:
     - Margin Money Subsidy: **25% in urban areas**, **35% in rural areas** for special category beneficiaries (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Ex-servicemen/PH/Minorities).
     - Maximum Project Cost: Up to ₹50 Lakhs for Manufacturing; Up to ₹20 Lakhs for Service/Trading sector.
     - Beneficiary own contribution: Only 5% to 10% of total project cost.
   - **Eligibility**: Any individual above 18 years, Self Help Groups, Cooperative Societies.

3. **Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)**
   - **Ministry**: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
   - **Official Portal**: https://www.mudra.org.in/
   - **Scheme Guidelines PDF**: https://www.mudra.org.in/Offerings
   - **Categories**:
     - **Shishu**: Loans up to ₹50,000 (ideal for micro-stalls, pushcart vendors, village artisans).
     - **Kishore**: Loans from ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 (ideal for established kirana stores, agri-trading stalls).
     - **Tarun**: Loans from ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 (for expansion, transport vehicles, storage equipment).
   - **Key Benefits**: Collateral-free loans, low processing fee, MUDRA Card issued for working capital overdraft.

4. **Stand-Up India Scheme**
   - **Ministry**: Department of Financial Services (DFS) & SIDBI
   - **Official Portal**: https://www.standupmitra.in/
   - **Guidelines PDF**: https://www.standupmitra.in/Home/SUISchemes
   - **Key Benefits**: Bank loans between ₹10 Lakhs and ₹1 Crore for at least one SC/ST and one Woman entrepreneur per bank branch for setting up greenfield enterprise in manufacturing, services, agri-allied, or trading.

5. **PM Formalisation of Micro food processing Enterprises (PMFME)**
   - **Ministry**: Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)
   - **Official Portal**: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/
   - **Guidelines PDF**: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/pmfme/assets/docs/PMFME_Scheme_Guidelines.pdf
   - **Key Benefits**: Credit-linked capital subsidy at 35% of eligible project cost with a maximum ceiling of ₹10 Lakh per unit. Seed capital of ₹40,000 per SHG member for working capital and small tools.

6. **Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)**
   - **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW)
   - **Official Portal**: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in/
   - **Guidelines PDF**: https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in/Home/Guidelines
   - **Key Benefits**: 3% per annum interest subvention on loans up to ₹2 Crores for 7 years. Credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE. Covers post-harvest management infrastructure, cold storage, ripening chambers, sorting & grading units.

7. **Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)**
   - **Ministry**: MoMSME & SIDBI
   - **Official Portal**: https://www.cgtmse.in/
   - **Key Benefits**: Collateral-free credit facility up to ₹500 Lakhs (₹5 Crores) to MSE borrowers extended by Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) without third-party guarantee.

8. **Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)**
   - **Ministry**: Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD)
   - **Official Portal**: https://aajeevika.gov.in/
   - **Key Benefits**: Interest subvention for Women SHGs on bank credit up to ₹3 Lakhs to bring effective interest rate down to 7% per annum. Revolving Fund of ₹20,000 - ₹30,000 per SHG.
